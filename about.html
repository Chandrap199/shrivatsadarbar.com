✨ Our Story

A Journey That Began at Home

"यत्र भक्तिः तत्र भगवान्।"

Where there is true devotion, there is the Divine.

Every journey has a beginning. Ours began at home.

Growing up, I watched my mother lovingly care for Laddu Gopal Ji every single day. It was never just a ritual — it was an expression of unconditional love and devotion.

Every morning she would gently wake Him, lovingly dress Him in fresh Poshaks, offer bhog with devotion, decorate Him beautifully, and care for Him with the same affection and dedication that a mother gives to her own children.

Her journey of seva began with a single Bal Gopal. As the years passed, her devotion only deepened, and today she lovingly serves three Laddu Gopal Jis.

Each one is cared for with the same love, attention and affection, becoming an inseparable part of our family and our daily lives.

"Why don't we dedicate ourselves to serving devotees by creating beautiful Shringar for Laddu Gopal Ji?"

That single thought planted the seed that would later become ShriVatsaDarbar.

Inspired by her unwavering faith, we began our journey with one simple purpose — to help devotees across India adorn their beloved Laddu Gopal Ji with handcrafted Poshaks, Mukuts, jewellery, Jhulas and divine accessories.

Today, every creation from ShriVatsaDarbar carries the same spirit we witnessed at home — to serve with sincerity, create with care, and honour devotion through every stitch, every ornament and every offering.
