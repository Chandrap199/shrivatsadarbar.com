Shipping Policy

We carefully prepare and dispatch every ShriVatsaDarbar order so your Laddu Gopal Shringar reaches you safely and beautifully.

Last Updated: 8 August 2026
01

Shipping Charges

We offer simple and transparent shipping charges for orders delivered across India.

Orders of ₹999 or more FREE Shipping
Orders below ₹999 Flat ₹99 Shipping

The applicable shipping charge will be communicated during the ordering process.

02

Delivery Across India

ShriVatsaDarbar currently offers delivery across India through trusted logistics and courier partners.

Courier partners may vary depending on the delivery location, service availability, package requirements and operational considerations.

We do not guarantee a particular courier partner for any individual order.

03

Order Processing & Dispatch

Orders are generally prepared and dispatched within 1–2 business days after order confirmation.

Orders containing customised products, special requirements or festive collections may require additional preparation time.

If additional time is required, our team will communicate the expected timeline with the customer.

04

Estimated Delivery Time

Once dispatched, orders are generally delivered within approximately 3–7 business days, depending on the delivery location.

Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary due to the destination, courier operations, weather conditions, public holidays or other circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

05

Delivery Address

Customers are responsible for providing an accurate and complete delivery address and a valid mobile number at the time of placing an order.

Please carefully verify your address, PIN code and contact details before confirming your order.

Delays or failed delivery attempts caused by an incorrect or incomplete address may require additional coordination before the order can be delivered.

06

Delivery Delays

While we make every reasonable effort to deliver orders within the estimated timeframe, unexpected delays may occur.

These may include severe weather, transportation disruptions, public holidays, operational delays, incorrect address information or other circumstances outside our control.

If we become aware of a significant delay, we will make reasonable efforts to assist you with the available shipment information.

07

Damaged or Incorrect Delivery

We carefully inspect and pack our products before dispatch.

If you receive a damaged, incorrect or materially different product, please contact us as soon as possible with photographs and relevant order details.

Please refer to our Return & Refund Policy for further information.

08

Shipping Communication

Order and delivery-related communication may be provided through WhatsApp, telephone, email or other communication details shared by the customer.

Customers may contact our team if they require assistance regarding their order or delivery.
