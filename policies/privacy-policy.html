Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your information with care, transparency and responsibility.

Last Updated: 8 August 2026
01

Introduction

ShriVatsaDarbar respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, store and protect information when you visit our website, communicate with us, enquire about our products or place an order.

ShriVatsaDarbar is based in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, India and primarily serves customers across India.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with ShriVatsaDarbar, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us.

Information you provide

  • Your name
  • Mobile or WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • Shipping and delivery address
  • Product, size or custom-order requirements
  • Messages, enquiries or other information you choose to provide

We only request information that is reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, process your order, provide customer support or fulfil delivery.

03

Website Usage Information

When you browse our website, certain technical information may be processed by the website infrastructure or hosting services, such as basic browser, device, network or access information.

We use such information primarily to operate, maintain, secure and improve the website.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through ordinary website browsing.

04

How We Use Your Information

Information provided to ShriVatsaDarbar may be used for purposes including:

  • Responding to product and customer enquiries
  • Helping you select the appropriate product or size
  • Processing and confirming orders
  • Communicating regarding your order
  • Arranging product delivery
  • Providing customer support
  • Handling custom or bulk-order requests
  • Improving our website, products and customer experience
  • Preventing misuse, fraud or security issues
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements
05

WhatsApp & Communication

ShriVatsaDarbar currently uses WhatsApp as an important channel for customer enquiries, order discussions and customer support.

When you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, information that you voluntarily provide through that communication may be used to respond to your enquiry or process your order.

WhatsApp is operated by Meta Platforms, Inc. and its applicable affiliates. Your use of WhatsApp is also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices.

06

Orders & Payments

Orders may be discussed and confirmed through WhatsApp or other communication channels provided by ShriVatsaDarbar.

We may collect information necessary to confirm your order and arrange fulfilment, including your name, contact details, delivery address and product requirements.

ShriVatsaDarbar does not currently use Razorpay or Stripe as its payment gateway. Payment instructions, where applicable, will be communicated during the ordering process.

Customers should not send unnecessary sensitive financial information through WhatsApp, email or website enquiry forms.

07

Shipping & Delivery

To fulfil an order, we may need to share necessary delivery information with trusted logistics, courier or delivery service providers.

Depending on the order, this may include information such as:

  • Recipient name
  • Delivery address
  • Mobile number
  • Order and shipment information

Such information is shared only to the extent reasonably necessary to fulfil and deliver your order.

08

Website Analytics

ShriVatsaDarbar may use website analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, to understand website traffic and improve the customer experience.

If Google Analytics is enabled on our website, it may collect information about website interactions and may use first-party cookies or similar technologies for analytics purposes.

We will configure analytics in a manner intended to avoid sending information such as names, email addresses or mobile numbers to analytics services.

Google Analytics is a service provided by Google. Its collection and processing of information is also subject to Google's applicable policies and terms.

09

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies where required for website functionality, security, analytics or other legitimate website operations.

We do not currently operate a newsletter platform or intentionally use cookies for behavioural advertising.

If we introduce additional advertising, analytics or tracking technologies in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain the relevant practices.

10

Third-Party Services

We may rely on third-party services to operate parts of our website, communicate with customers, analyse website performance or deliver orders.

Depending on the services actually used by ShriVatsaDarbar, these may include website hosting providers, communication platforms, analytics providers and logistics or courier partners.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

11

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information entrusted to us against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, disclosure or loss.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

If we become aware of a security incident affecting personal information, we will take appropriate steps as required under applicable law.

12

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including order fulfilment, customer support, business records, dispute resolution, security and compliance with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer reasonably required, we may delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

13

Your Privacy Choices & Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may contact ShriVatsaDarbar regarding personal information that you have provided to us.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable legal requirements, you may request:

  • Information about the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion of personal information where permitted
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent
  • Assistance with privacy-related concerns or complaints

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the details provided below.

14

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for general consumers and are not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is restricted by applicable law.

15

Changes to This Privacy Policy

ShriVatsaDarbar may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business, website functionality, services, technology or applicable legal requirements.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
